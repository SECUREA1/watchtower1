Lockpick
Intel and doors can be locked. Approach and press X (or Pie → Hack/Lock) to open lockpick (some locks auto-trigger on contact). Blink/Pinch/Space/Left-Click to set pins when the dot is inside the gold window. HOLD SHIFT (or hold pinch) for Tension (slows pins, adds stress). Abort with Backspace/Esc (may bend a pick if stress is high). Inventory uses Lockpicks; lock failures can break a pick.
