Configure players (1–4) and select a cue skin for each player. Players take turns on each hole; the hole advances once all players are holed out.
Select one of four unique gameplay themes. This affects visuals and course mechanics across the 18-hole round.
Theme mechanics overview:
Classic: balanced.
Neon Rush: boost pads (speed-up zones) + glow.
Ice Drift: lower friction + livelier bounces.
Desert Storm: gusty wind + more sand drag zones.
