How to Play — Casino Rules

Objective

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Number cards = value. J/Q/K = 10. Ace = 1 or 11 (best use).

  • Hit — take a card
  • Stand — finish your turn
  • Double — double bet (first two cards only), one card, then stand

Rules & Payouts

  • 6-deck shoe (auto reshuffle)
  • Dealer stands on soft 17
  • Blackjack pays 3:2
  • Dealer peeks for blackjack; dealer blackjack ends round immediately
