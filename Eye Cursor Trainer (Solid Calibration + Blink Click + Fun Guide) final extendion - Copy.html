Guide: Use the Training Mode

Run the wizard to calibrate gaze + blink. Then start challenges to learn smooth movement and reliable clicking.

Tip: Lighting + Head Stability

Look straight at the camera, keep your head steady, and avoid strong backlight. Calibration uses stability gating.

Tip: Blink Click Mode

Long blink is the most reliable. Double-blink is supported after validation. You can also use dwell click.
