🏀 Hoops Hub

A single launcher that ties together your different gameplay modes (AI scrimmage, first-person challenges, pinch & toss).
Camera note: For camera access, run over https or localhost.

AI Scrimmage — 2v2 (fast)

Team play with AI, passing, steals, shot clock, and camera modes. Smaller roster = quicker decision-making.

Full Court 5v5 (original)

Your original full-court 5v5 build (kept for comparison / regression testing).

First-Person — Dribble + Steal Defender

Close-up handling focus: dribble control, steals, shot-clock pressure.

First-Person — Auto Pickup + Fist Lock + Open Throw

Quick pick-up loop with fist-lock aiming and open-hand release throws.

Pinch & Toss — 3D Player + Shoot/Pass

Pinch-based grab / toss mechanics for training ball control.

Tip

On most modes, a full reset is safest after changing camera permissions or switching devices.

If you host these files from a local dev server, keep them in the same folder so the launcher can load them via relative paths. In the AI Scrimmage mode you can also change roster in-game via Matchup.
